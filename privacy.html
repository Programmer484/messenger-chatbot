Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how the Messenger Chatbot (“the App”) collects, uses, and protects information obtained through Facebook Messenger interactions. This App is operated by Ryan (“we,” “our,” or “us”) in Canada.

1. Information We Collect

When you or your tenants use the App, we may collect the following data:

  • Facebook user profile information (such as name, profile picture, and Facebook ID) when users interact with the chatbot via Facebook Messenger.
  • Messages and conversations sent via Facebook Messenger between landlords and tenants, handled by the chatbot to automate responses and record communication history.

2. How We Use the Information

The information collected is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To deliver automated responses on behalf of landlords via Messenger.
  • To help landlords manage communications and store conversation history with tenants.
  • To maintain and improve the App’s functionality.

3. Data Storage and Processing

Data collected through the App may be stored and processed using Supabase, a secure third-party cloud service. Supabase acts only as a data processor and does not use or share data for its own purposes.

4. Data Retention

Tenant and landlord communication data is retained only as long as the landlord requires it to manage tenancy-related communications. All stored data is permanently deleted within 30 days once it is no longer needed.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade any personal information. Data is shared only with trusted service providers (such as Supabase) who help us operate the App securely and efficiently. These providers are bound by confidentiality and data protection agreements.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. User Rights and Data Deletion

Users may request access to or deletion of their data at any time by contacting us at Ryanleo2006@gmail.com. We will respond to all deletion requests within a reasonable timeframe, typically within 30 days.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date.

9. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact Ryan at Ryanleo2006@gmail.com.
